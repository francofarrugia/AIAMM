AI agents for concentrated liquidity

AIAMM trained reinforcement-learning agents to manage a Uniswap v3 liquidity position on their own. On five years of real ETH/USDC prices they did not beat leaving the position alone. They pulled ahead only when markets became very volatile, and so far that has been shown in simulation.

When does active management pay?

Each agent is scored by alpha: its final portfolio value minus that of a passive full-range position on the same price path, after fees and gas. Below zero, doing nothing was better.

PPO (−$7.09) and SAC (−$7.11) trailed DDQN on real data; all three shortfalls are statistically significant after Bonferroni correction. Real ETH/USDC prices trend, and a trend rewards a position that never leaves its range. Each rebalance costs gas and forgoes fees, and in calm or trending markets the agents did not earn that back.

How the agents work

What they see

15 hourly inputs: price and pool returns, volatility, where the price sits in the range, how much capital is active, fees earned, momentum and a price-based sentiment indicator.

What they can do

Five actions: hold; add or remove 10% of capital; move the ±40% range up or down by 3%. Each move pays $0.15–$0.30 in modelled gas.

How they learn

Double DQN, PPO and SAC, trained over 1,000 episodes with a curriculum of 100, 300 and 500 hours, rewarded for beating the passive position.

Running on a public blockchain

On 28 October 2026 the trained DDQN agent ran a 48-hour episode on the Ethereum Sepolia testnet with no human in the loop, using the official Uniswap v3 contracts and two test tokens with no monetary value. Each hour the pool was moved to the next real ETH/USDC price from 26–28 February 2026; the agent read the chain and acted. It held 44 times and removed liquidity 4 times.

It finished at $1,932.43 against $1,934.07 for the passive position: alpha −$1.64, effectively a tie in a calm window, as the simulation predicts. One episode on test tokens shows the system works; it is not a performance test.

A lesson for anyone deploying agents. Twice a withdrawal succeeded on-chain but the public RPC failed before its receipt could be read. The script retried, collected nothing, and its own bookkeeping showed a $331 loss. Reconciling every step against the chain's own state corrected it to −$1.64. The published kit now never re-sends a broadcast transaction and includes the reconciliation script.

What this study does not show

The agents' sentiment input is calculated from price momentum; no news or social-media text was analysed, and removing the input changed nothing (p = 0.88). Prices come from the Binance exchange, so pool volume and fees are simulated. Slippage was not measured. The positive high-volatility result has not been tested with real money on a live market. Those are the next steps for this line of research.

Reproduce it

The repository holds the final V13 simulation, the hourly ETH/USDC dataset, the October 2026 supplementary analysis and the Sepolia kit. Anyone with free Sepolia test ETH can repeat the on-chain run in about an hour.
